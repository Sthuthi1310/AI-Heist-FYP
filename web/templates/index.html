What is Model Fidelity?

Fidelity measures the percentage of test cases where the attacker's clone gives the exact same answer as your real model. High fidelity (e.g. >80%) means your intellectual property was stolen! Defenses drop fidelity to protect your AI.

🛡️ Rate Limiting (Token Bucket)

Throttles high-speed automated bots with HTTP 429 "Too Many Requests". Real users browsing normally are never blocked.

Analyzes sub-human request velocity, burst frequency, and systematic decision boundary probing.

Infects queries flagged as bots with controlled label flips and probability noise, poisoning the thief's training dataset.

Defense Evaluation Summary: Attack Thwarted

In a representative run, enabling API defenses dropped observed copycat agreement from 84.9% to 58.2% (-26.7 percentage points). Rate limiting throttled bot extraction bursts and adaptive noise poisoned stolen responses. Genuine-user utility has not yet been separately measured.
